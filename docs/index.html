Research

I am a non-Native Ph.D. student in the Department of Sociology at the University of Oregon studying environmental sociology. I utilize mixed methods approaches, including quantitative, network, and content analyses to understand how settler colonialism structures relationships between humans and the natural world. Specifically, I am interested in decolonization, environmental movements, Indigenous-settler relations, environmental health, and industrial agriculture. I received a B.A. in Sociology (Hons) with concentrations in Statistics and Environmental Studies from Grinnell College in 2025.
